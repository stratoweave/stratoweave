# Security policy

## Report a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities in StratoWeave or its development
process privately to [security@stratoweave.org](mailto:security@stratoweave.org).
If you are unsure whether an issue is a vulnerability, contact us there anyway.
Do not disclose vulnerabilities in public issues, pull requests, or discussions
before we have had an opportunity to investigate and coordinate a fix.

Include what you know:

- The affected component and version or commit.
- A description of the issue, its security impact, and any conditions needed to
  exploit it.
- Steps to reproduce it or a minimal proof of concept, with relevant logs.
- Whether it is already public or being exploited, and any relevant links.
- Your preferred disclosure timeline and whether and how you would like to be
  credited.

Please explain the impact of any scanner findings; raw tool output alone usually
does not provide enough information to investigate. Remove credentials, personal
data, and other secrets from examples and logs. If sensitive details need extra
protection, ask us how to share them before sending them.

## How we handle reports

The maintainers will acknowledge your report, investigate it, and keep you
informed of the outcome and next steps. We will handle non-public reports in
confidence, sharing details only with people needed to investigate, fix, or
coordinate the issue, including affected dependency maintainers when necessary.
If we determine that the issue is not a vulnerability, we will explain why.

We will work with you to agree on a disclosure date, allowing time to prepare a
fix or mitigation. Please allow up to 90 days from the initial report for this
coordination; this is not a guaranteed fix deadline. Active exploitation or
existing public disclosure may require earlier publication. If you do not
receive a response, please follow up at the same address.

When disclosing a confirmed vulnerability, we will publish an advisory in the
[repository's security advisories](https://github.com/stratoweave/stratoweave/security/advisories)
with its impact, affected versions, and available fixes or mitigations, and seek
a CVE identifier where appropriate. We will credit you unless you prefer to
remain anonymous.

This policy follows the [Linux Foundation's vulnerability reporting guidance](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/security).
